# Privacy — Stretch 2.0

Updated September 22, 2026.

Stretch has no accounts, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or cloud service. Fonts, illustrations, and exercise guidance are bundled locally.

Preferences, completed break counts, movement minutes, and recent exercise IDs are stored in a local JSON file. Daily history is limited to 365 entries.

- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Stretch/config.json`
- Windows: `%APPDATA%/Stretch/config.json`

Source and privacy links open in your browser only when selected. Those websites and download/hosting providers have their own privacy policies. Stretch 2.0 does not automatically check for updates. The website has no analytics, tracking cookies, remote fonts, or accounts; its demo runs locally in the browser.

Copy diagnostics copies app/runtime versions, platform, and reminder state to your clipboard. It includes no exercise history and sends nothing.

Quit Stretch and delete its configuration file to reset preferences and progress. Uninstalling the app may leave that file behind.
