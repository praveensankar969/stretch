GOOD TO KNOW

A few little
answers.

How often should I take a break?

Stretch starts at 30 minutes, and you can choose 5–240 minutes. That’s a preference, not a medical rule. HSE recommends short, frequent changes of activity and suggests longer breaks away from the screen too. A short stretch doesn’t replace walking, exercise, or a suitable workstation.

Will it interrupt my focus?

Automatic reminders open as a small card without keyboard focus. They wait while your Mac is asleep, locked, or idle. You can set quiet hours, pause reminders, or snooze. Stretch does not detect meetings or macOS Focus.

What happens in fullscreen?

On Mac, the reminder can appear over fullscreen apps on your current Space. Turn this off in Preferences if you prefer. The movement player also has its own immersive view; press Escape to return to the small card.

Are the exercises right for everyone?

No movement suits everybody. These are short adaptations of public health guidance, with illustrative animations rather than clinical motion capture. Follow the written cues, stay in a comfortable range, and stop if it hurts. Ask your care professional if you have an injury or are unsure.

Is it really free?

Yes. Stretch is open source under the MIT license. No subscription, ads, or account. The 2.0 redesign focuses on Mac; older Windows releases remain available on GitHub.
