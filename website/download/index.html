Small beginnings.

Stretch 2.0 is available for Mac and Windows. Free, open source, and ready for a little room on your computer.

Get Stretch for Mac

Choose Apple Silicon for an M-series Mac, or Intel for a Mac with an Intel processor. Find your chip in Apple menu → About This Mac.

Download for Apple Silicon ↗ Download for Intel ↗

Version 2.0.0 · macOS · DMG installers

Open the DMG, drag Stretch into Applications, and launch it. Stretch lives in your menu bar.

Get Stretch for Windows

A little pause for your Windows desktop. Download the 64-bit installer to get started.

Download for Windows ↗

Version 2.0.0 · Windows (64-bit) · EXE installer

Open the EXE and follow the installer. Launch Stretch and find it in your system tray.

Make yourself at home

Choose your reminder interval and quiet hours; you can change both later.

For a build that hasn’t been notarized, macOS may ask you to approve it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Only open a build you trust. Release notes should state its signing and notarization status.
